## 📌 About the Project

The **CPI Inflation Analysis in India** project explores Consumer Price Index (CPI) trends across Rural, Urban, and Rural+Urban sectors from 2013 to 2023.

The project uses Microsoft Excel to clean and transform data, analyze category-wise price index trends, compare annual and monthly patterns, study pre- and post-COVID changes, and investigate correlations between fuel prices and other expenditure categories.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze CPI trends from 2013 to 2023.
- Compare price index trends across Rural, Urban, and Rural+Urban sectors.
- Study year-on-year changes in the General Index.
- Analyze monthly trends in food categories.
- Compare CPI trends before and after COVID-19.
- Examine fuel and transportation price index movements.
- Perform correlation analysis between fuel and selected expenditure categories.
- Present findings through Excel-based analysis and visualizations.

## 📈 Key Findings

### 1. General Index Trend

The annual average General Index for the Rural+Urban sector increased from approximately **129.20 in 2016 to 177.62 in 2023**, indicating an overall rise in the consumer price index level.

### 2. Sector-Wise Comparison

The analysis compares Rural, Urban, and Rural+Urban index values to identify differences in price trends across sectors.

### 3. Food Inflation Analysis

Monthly and annual comparisons examine categories such as cereals, milk, vegetables, fruits, pulses, and other food items to identify variations in their index values.

### 4. Pre- and Post-COVID Analysis

The project compares trends in food and beverages, household goods and services, and health from 2013 to 2023 to understand how their index levels changed over time.

### 5. Fuel and Transportation

The workbook examines the relationship between fuel and light and transport and communication index values.

### 6. Correlation Analysis

The workbook reports the following correlations:

| Category Comparison | Correlation |
|---|---:|
| Fuel vs. Non-alcoholic Beverages | 0.689 |
| Fuel vs. Transport and Communication | 0.662 |
| Fuel vs. Food and Beverages | -0.199 |
| Fuel vs. Cereals and Products | -0.239 |

These results indicate positive associations between fuel and non-alcoholic beverages, and between fuel and transportation. The other two comparisons show weak negative associations in the analyzed data.

*Note: Correlation does not imply causation. CPI index values are not the same as inflation rates; percentage changes in the index over a defined period are needed to measure inflation.*
